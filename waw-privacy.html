Worship & the Word

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 28, 2026

This policy explains exactly what Worship & the Word collects, why, and what we do with it. The app is built around a simple commitment: collect as little as possible, never sell or share what we collect, and be plain about both.

What we collect and why

Anonymous device identifier

When you first open the app, we generate a random identifier on your device and store it in your phone's secure storage. This identifier is sent to our database (Supabase) so the app can:

  • Remember your translation preference (BSB, KJV, NET, NLT)
  • Remember your notification time preference
  • Remember which devotionals you've saved as favorites
  • Remember whether you've supported the app with an optional donation

The identifier is not tied to your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information. It identifies your install of the app — not you.

App preferences and saved devotionals

  • Your selected Bible translation
  • Whether daily notifications are enabled
  • The time you've chosen for daily notifications
  • A list of devotional IDs you've saved as favorites
  • Whether you've made a one-time donation to support the app

These are stored in our database under your anonymous device identifier and sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

On-device only (never sent to our servers)

  • Whether you've completed the onboarding screen
  • Whether the daily-notification opt-in card has been shown to you

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera
  • We do not use analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or display ads of any kind
  • We do not profile you or sell any data to anyone, ever

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — database hosting for the items listed above. Supabase privacy policy
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time donation. We never see your payment details. Google privacy policy
  • YouTube — worship songs are embedded via YouTube. YouTube applies its own data practices to playback. YouTube privacy policy

Children's privacy

The app is appropriate for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete any associated identifier.

Data retention

The data described above is kept as long as your install identifier is in our database. If you uninstall the app, the identifier on your device is destroyed, but the corresponding row in our database may remain. To request deletion, email us — we will remove it within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Delete all on-device data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings
  • Request deletion of all data associated with your install by contacting us — we comply within 30 days
  • Stop optional donations at any time — they are one-time, non-subscription gifts

Security

  • All data sent between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS (TLS)
  • Our database encrypts data at rest
  • The anonymous device identifier is stored in your phone's secure system keystore
